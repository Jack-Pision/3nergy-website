# 3NERGY — Image Prompts for Gemini / Chatbot Image Generators

This guide contains **ready-to-copy prompts** specifically tailored for **Gemini, Nano, Imagen 3, and conversational chatbot image generators**.

Unlike Midjourney or Stable Diffusion, chatbot generators don't use CLI flags (like `--ar 4:5`, `--style raw`, or `--no`). Instead, they produce the best results when given **descriptive natural language, clear photographic camera directions, tangible material textures, and explicit negative constraints embedded directly in prose**.

---

## Chatbot Prompting Rules for Best Results
1. **Copy the entire prompt block** inside each section below and paste it directly into your Gemini / chatbot conversation.
2. **Aspect Ratios**: If your chatbot UI has an aspect ratio selector, pick the recommended ratio listed (e.g. 4:5, 4:3, 16:9). If not, the natural language prompt already guides the model on framing and orientation.
3. **Typography**: The prompts instruct the model to produce clean, minimal typography ("3NERGY") or leave labels unprinted to prevent garbled AI text.

## 6. How to Save and Deploy Images into 3NERGY

When your chatbot generates each image:
1. **Download the image** in the highest resolution available (at least 1200px wide).
2. **Convert/optimize to WebP or PNG** (aim for 80-180 KB for cards, ~300 KB for hero banners).
3. **Save into `public/images/`** with the exact filename specified above (e.g., `card1_full.png`, `routine_couple.png`).
4. Next.js will automatically hot-reload and display your new photography on the website!
